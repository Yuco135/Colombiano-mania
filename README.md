# Colombiano-Mania!
Explore the world of Colombia and play exciting top down mini games all across Colombia that all are unique.

# Story
Your stuck in Medillin, colombia and must reach Bogota, Colombia for your mother's birthday in three days. Now you musdt traverse all through Colombia to reach Bogota. Will you be able to reach your mother's birthday in time. 

# Learn Colombia's Culture
Learn all about Colombia's vibrate culture like:
- The amazing coffee
- food
- sports
- dance/salsa
